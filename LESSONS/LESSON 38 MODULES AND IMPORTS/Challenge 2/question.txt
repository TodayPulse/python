Advanced Practice Tasks: Circular Dependencies & Dependency Injection
To truly master the architectural patterns needed to defeat circular imports, complete
 these three targeted tasks building directly on Challenge 1.

Task 1: Refactoring a Two-Way Notification & Payment Pipeline
Scenario: You have two modules in your coffee enterprise app: payment.py and notifications.py.

payment.py needs to trigger an email confirmation via notifications.py once a transaction
 succeeds.

notifications.py needs to query payment.py to check the account balance or transaction 
status before sending billing alerts.
This causes a cyclic dependency crash if imported globally.

Your Task:

Refactor both modules so that neither imports the other at the top-level module scope.

Implement Dependency Injection by passing a callable notification function (or callback) 
into the payment processing function.

Write a main.py script that orchestrates both modules cleanly without triggering an 
ImportError.
